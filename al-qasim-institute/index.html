About Al Qasim Institute

Every Child Deserves a Chance to Shine

For decades, Al Qasim Institute for Special Children has stood beside families in Jhelum, offering a warm and nurturing home where children with special needs are seen, valued, and given room to grow. Through specialised education, therapeutic care, and skills training, we help every child build the confidence and independence they need for a dignified future.

Our dedicated teachers, therapists, and caregivers work hand-in-hand with families to design individual plans around each child's strengths — because we believe ability, not disability, should define a child's story.
