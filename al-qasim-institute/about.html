About Al Qasim Institute

A trusted home of learning, care, and hope for special children in Jhelum since our founding.

Our Story

A Legacy Built on Compassion

Al Qasim Institute for Special Children was founded in Jhelum with a simple but powerful conviction: every child, regardless of ability, deserves the chance to learn, grow, and belong. What began as a small community effort to support local families has grown into a dedicated institute offering specialised education, therapy, and care to children with a wide range of special needs.

Our Mission

To provide quality education, care, support, and opportunities that empower special children to live with dignity, confidence, and independence.

Our Vision

A society where every special child is valued, supported, and given equal opportunities to achieve their full potential.

Our Approach

How We Support Every Child

A whole-child philosophy that combines education, therapy, and family partnership.

Individual Care

Every child receives a personalised plan built around their unique strengths and needs.

Inclusive Learning

Classrooms designed to welcome every learning style and ability with patience and encouragement.

Skills Development

Practical life and vocational skills that build independence beyond the classroom.

Family Support

Guidance, counselling, and resources that empower parents as partners in their child's growth.

A Safe Environment

A secure, welcoming campus where every child feels protected, respected, and at home.

Why We Need Your Support

Sustained by Generosity, Driven by Purpose

Al Qasim Institute operates as a charitable organisation and depends entirely on the generosity of donors, sponsors, and well-wishers to continue providing free or subsidised education, therapy, meals, transport, and care to children who would otherwise have nowhere to turn. Every contribution — large or small — helps us keep our doors open and our promise to these children unbroken.
