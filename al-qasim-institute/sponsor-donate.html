Where Your Donation Goes

Every donation made to Al Qasim Institute for Special Children directly supports the welfare, education, care, and development of our children. Funds are used responsibly to cover teaching resources, therapeutic care, meals, transport, uniforms, and facility upkeep — with careful stewardship at every step, so your generosity creates the greatest possible impact.

Education & Therapy

Teaching resources, specialist staff and therapy sessions.

Meals & Welfare

Nutritious meals and everyday care for every child.

Transport & Uniforms

Safe transport to and from the institute, plus uniforms.

Facilities Upkeep

Maintaining a safe, well-equipped learning environment.
